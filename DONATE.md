# Support MiniApps

[English] · [Español](DONATE_ES.md)

MiniApps are free mini-games and utilities: MIT licensed, no ads, no tracking, no accounts and no
backend — so they make no money and never will. There is no paid tier, nothing behind a wall, and
donating gets you nothing extra. If one of them was useful and you want to say thanks, this page
explains how.

Anything sent goes to the maintainer, [Carlos Piñán](https://github.com/cpinan), personally. This
is not a company or a charity, and donations are **not tax deductible**.

---

## Free ways to help (worth more than money)

- ⭐ **Star the repo** — [cpinan/MiniApps](https://github.com/cpinan/MiniApps)
- 🔗 **Share the link** — [cpinan.github.io/MiniApps](https://cpinan.github.io/MiniApps/)
- 🧩 **Send a mini-app** — each one is a self-contained folder under `apps/`, no build step and no
  dependencies, so a new app is a complete contribution
- 🐞 **File a good bug report** — browser, OS, what you expected, what happened, and the build
  stamp shown at the bottom of the panel
- 🌍 **Translate them** — they are Spanish-only today

every month, and there is nothing here to deliver.

| Method | How | Notes |

personal number to everyone who reads this file.

## What is not needed

- **You never need to donate to use anything.** Everything is published and fully working, free.
- **There is no "pro" tier**, no locked features, no watermark, no participant limit.
- **Nothing asks inside the app.** There is a quiet coffee icon in the header that opens this page,
  and nothing else: no dialogs, no banners, no reminders, no counting how often you used it.
